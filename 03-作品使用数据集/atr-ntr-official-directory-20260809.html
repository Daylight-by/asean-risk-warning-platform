NTR - ASEAN Member States' National Trade Repositories

 

 

 

Brunei Darussalam		 Welcome to the Brunei Darussalam National Trade Repository. It is an online web portal that provide access to trade related information such as import tariffs, preferential tariffs, rules of origin, non-tariff measures, procedures and documentary requirements.
www.bdntr.gov.bn
 

Cambodia
Welcome to the Cambodian National Trade Repository! One click towards all your trade-related information needs.


 
www.cambodiantr.gov.kh

Indonesia		 The INTR is Indonesia’s Single Reference National Trade Repository, part of Indonesia’s National Single Window System, which contains references to import/export trade regulation and related information.
 
www.insw.go.id/intr
 

Lao PDR
This website will help traders find all the information they require to import goods into Laos and export goods from Laos.


 

www.laotradeportal.gov.la

Malaysia
Welcome to Malaysia’s National Trade Repository (MNTR) and your trade information gateway.


 
mytraderepository.customs.gov.my
 

Myanmar		 Welcome to Myanmar’s National Trade Repository, which provides traders and policy makers with all relevant information on trade laws and regulations and which aims at facilitating imports to and exports from Myanmar.
 
www.myanmartradeportal.gov.mm
 

Philippines
Welcome to the Philippine National Trade Repository! Everything you need to know about trading in the Philippines all in one convenient location.

 
pntr.gov.ph
 

Singapore		 Singapore’s National Trade Repository (NTR) is an online portal aimed at providing traders with information on Singapore’s free trade agreements, custom laws and procedures, and other trade-related information.
 
www.fta.gov.sg
 

Thailand
Welcome to the Thailand National Trade Repository or Thailand NTR.This website is an information gateway on Thailand’s international trade regulations.

 

www.thailandntr.com
 

Vietnam		 Welcome to Viet Nam’s National Trade Repository (NTR). This gateway provides access to the available sources of information related to the international trade measures and regulations of Viet Nam.
ntr.moit.gov.vn
